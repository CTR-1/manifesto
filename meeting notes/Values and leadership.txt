## principles of organization: fundamental rules an guidelines used to organize people and solve problems
  * decisions should be made at the most local level possible.
  * Everyone who wants to speak should get to speak, because the answers to problems come from unexpected places.
  * people have rightful claim to the products of their labor
  * every living thing has right to self defense
  * pay, or shares from production should not exceed a agreed upon ratio. no CEO to worker pay ratio insanity. benefits of shared labor should be distributed with equity.
  * decisions should be made with transparency
  * law and rules must be in common language that everyone can understand
  * simpler is better
  * moral and lawful authority is derived from consent
  * do not rush to violence
  * power should not be focused in the hands of few
  * kill them with kindness
  * representatives should be accountable to the people they represent at a physical level. no hiding where you stay at.....
  * desicions should be made by representatives chosen through stages of selection
  * no process of administration should be opaque to the public. no counting ballots in secrets. no secret hearings. no secret courts.
  * justice must be equally available to all people regardless of economic or personal circumstances
  * profession should be by experience and reputation, not regulation. license should be to improve reputation, not bar entry
  * administrative and representative bodies must not bar people from economic activity
  * there cannot be a professional legal class
  * there cannot be a professional law enforcment class
  * government positions must be rotated like jury duty.
  * credit or value should not be created arbitrarily
  * trust must be earned
  * descisions must not be made based on the assummption that people are telling the truth. truth is determined with evidence.
  * defense is shared responsibility
